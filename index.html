What is Plug & Play Solar?

Plug-in solar, sometimes called balcony solar or balcony PV, provides households with a small solar generation system designed primarily for household self-consumption.

1

Generate

One or two solar panels convert daylight into renewable DC electricity.

2

Convert

A compatible microinverter converts the electricity into AC electricity suitable for household use.

3

Use

Electricity generated during daylight can reduce electricity imported from the grid.

Plug-in Solar in Ireland

Ireland is developing a dedicated regulatory pathway for small plug-in solar systems.

Current status - November 2026

Ireland's simplified plug-and-play framework is not yet operational. Customers should not assume that a European plug-in solar kit can currently be connected directly to an Irish household socket.

What's expected in 2027?

Ireland is working towards a simplified pathway for compliant small plug-in solar systems. The market opening remains subject to completion of the necessary legislation, safety requirements, standards and network arrangements.

Plug & Play Solar will only supply systems for grid-connected plug-in use in Ireland when that use is permitted and the relevant equipment requirements have been confirmed.

What Could an 800W Solar Kit Produce?

A well-positioned 800Wp solar panel array in Ireland could typically generate approximately 650-800 kWh per year. Actual output depends on location, orientation, shading, panel angle and inverter efficiency.

Illustrative example

At 700 kWh annual generation and 70% direct self-consumption, 490 kWh would be used in the home. At an illustrative electricity price of €0.35/kWh, that is about €171.50 per year.

Illustration only. Actual generation, tariffs, self-consumption, equipment cost and savings vary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is plug-in solar currently legal in Ireland?

Ireland does not yet have the simplified plug-and-play framework envisaged for 2027. Consumers should wait for confirmation of the final Irish rules before connecting a plug-in generating system to a household socket.

When will Plug & Play Solar kits become available?

We intend to supply suitable systems once Ireland's regulatory framework permits their use and the relevant technical and product requirements have been confirmed.

Will I need an electrician?

The proposed simplified framework is intended to make compliant small systems easier to connect. The final Irish connection and safety requirements must be confirmed.
