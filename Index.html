Irish Electrical Compliance & ESB Rules

While 800W "Balkonkraftwerk" plug-in solar kits are popular in Germany and across parts of Europe, Ireland operates under strict national electrical safety standards.

1

No 3-Pin Socket Feed-In

Under Irish wiring rules (I.S. 10101), plugging a solar microinverter directly into a standard 13A domestic wall socket is not permitted due to circuit overload and isolation risks.

2

ESB Networks NC6 Form

Any microgeneration system connected to the Irish electricity grid must comply with EN 50549-1 standards and be notified to ESB Networks via an NC6 application.

3

Safe Electric Certification

Fixed grid-connected solar installations in Ireland must be wired on a dedicated circuit and certified by a Safe Electric Registered Electrical Contractor (REC).
