Plug-in Solar in Ireland

A plain-English record of confirmed Irish developments, proposed changes and what they mean for households interested in plug-in solar.

Current position
Plug-in solar systems are not currently approved for connection and use under a simplified plug-and-play framework in Ireland. Plug & Play Solar does not currently offer plug-in solar systems for sale or use in Ireland.
24 NOVEMBER 2026 · GOVERNMENT UPDATE

Government publishes Plug-in Solar Working Group Options Report

The Department of Climate, Energy and the Environment has published the National Energy Affordability Report and Action Plan and welcomed the Plug-in Solar Working Group Options Report as an important step towards enabling plug-in solar in Ireland.

The working group, coordinated by ESB Networks and involving the Department, the National Standards Authority of Ireland (NSAI), the Commission for Regulation of Utilities (CRU) and other stakeholders, explored pathways for the safe and standardised deployment of plug-in solar for Irish domestic customers.

What it means: this is significant progress, but it does not mean a simplified plug-and-play framework is operational today. Implementation work remains necessary before households can rely on a new simplified connection route.

Primary source: Government of Ireland, Department of Climate, Energy and the Environment, 24 November 2026. Read the Government announcement.
NOVEMBER 2026 · EXPLAINER

What happens next for plug-in solar in Ireland?

The Options Report examines routes for enabling plug-in solar, but a report is not the same thing as an operational connection framework.

Before Plug & Play Solar begins selling systems for grid-connected plug-in use, we will check the final Irish requirements covering equipment standards, safety, network arrangements, registration or notification requirements and any applicable power limits.

Our approach is simple: we will describe proposals as proposals and only describe a requirement as final when the responsible Irish authorities have confirmed it.

UPDATED NOVEMBER 2026 · REGULATORY STATUS

Is plug-in solar currently approved in Ireland?

Not under the simplified plug-and-play framework currently being developed. Customers should not assume that a plug-in solar product sold elsewhere in Europe can simply be connected to an Irish household socket under a new simplified regime.

Plug & Play Solar intends to remain an information and future-supply site until the applicable Irish regulatory, technical and safety requirements are finalised and the intended use is permitted.
